# Marketing A/B Testing Analysis

## About the Project
This project analyzes the effectiveness of marketing advertisements using A/B Testing. It compares the conversion rates of two groups: Ad Group (Test Group) and PSA Group (Control Group).

## Objectives
- Compare Ad and PSA group conversion rates.
- Analyze conversion rates by advertising day.
- Analyze conversion rates by advertising hour.
- Perform Independent T-Test.
- Perform One-Way ANOVA.
- Identify statistically significant differences.

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Conversion Rate: Test vs Control
Compared the conversion rates between the Ad group and PSA group.

### 2. Conversion Rate by Day
Analyzed conversion rates across different advertising days.

### 3. Conversion Rate by Hour
Analyzed conversion patterns based on advertising hours.

### 4. Independent T-Test
Applied an independent two-sample t-test to compare conversion rates.

### 5. ANOVA Test
Used One-Way ANOVA to analyze conversion differences across advertising days and hours.
